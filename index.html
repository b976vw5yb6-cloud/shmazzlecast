SHMAZZLECAST

ShmazzleCast is the personal casting setup of ShmazzleTV for Orion Drift esports broadcasts. It runs on the caster's own PC: stream overlays, scene control and match graphics for OBS.

It can connect to the caster's own YouTube channel to schedule live streams for Orion Drift Competitive matches the caster has claimed. It is not offered to other users.

Streams: youtube.com/@ShmazzleTV
